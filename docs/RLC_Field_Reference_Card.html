Firmware 1.2.1, both units
Pre-fire 5 s · pulse 1 s · arm timeout 10 s
Full detail in RLC-OPS-001

AFiring sequence

  1. Check the remote. LINK OK not WEAK, band green SAFE, battery good. Grey band = pad state unknown. Stop.
  2. Select the correct channel. Double-check. ○ OPEN → arming will be refused.
  3. Call the pad clear. Get an acknowledgement back.
  4. LCO: key to ARM, then step back. KEY lamp on, RELAY LIVE still dark, band yellow. Relay lamp lit now → key to SAFE, unit out of service.
  5. LCO: arm switch to ARMED. Band orange READY TO ARM. Pad unchanged.
  6. Long-press the encoder, 500 ms. 2 beeps, band red ARM RELAY LIVE, siren on and stays on. 3 rapid beeps = refused. Read §C, fix, restart at 1.
  7. You now have 10 s. Not ready? Disarm and re-arm.
  8. Range clear → press and HOLD fire. Never tap it. Ring red, tone 4 Hz, 5 s countdown.
  9. Keep holding through the countdown and the 1 s pulse. Releasing from step 8 on cuts current.
  10. Release. FIRE COMPLETE, 10 s. Check igniter continuity: ○ OPEN – LIKELY FIRED expected.
  11. Fire the other pads if needed. Repeat from step 2.
  12. Remote arm switch to SAFE, key removed. Before anything else.
  13. Base arm key to SAFE and removed. Check the band is green. Only now approach the pad.
FIRE COMPLETE is not ignition It means the controller finished its pulse. Rocket still on the pad = misfire: go to §E, and do not shorten the wait.

BStatic motor test — what changes

  • Sequence identical — §A steps 1–13, unabbreviated.
  • Nobody on the thrust axis, either way. Base off-axis, behind the stand.
  • Distances sized for a case failure, not launch tables. RSO decides.
  • Stand, anchoring and recorders verified first, base off, cabling clear of the firing leads. Igniter fitted last.
  • Fire watch posted who is not the LCO. Area cleared and damped.
  • Hold position through the burn and cool-down. The pulse ends; the motor doesn’t.
  • Never “try it again.” A misfire leaves a loaded, restrained motor at ground level with an igniter that may still fire late. Restart from §E in full.

Aborting is free It costs one igniter at most. The cheapest action — letting go — is also the safest. If you are unsure, let go.

EMisfire drill

  1. Release. Do not re-fire.
  2. Disarm the remote. Key to SAFE, removed.
  3. Wait 60 s minimum (NAR / TRA) or your code’s figure. Time it.
  4. Read the igniter line first. ○ OPEN fired, motor didn’t light · ● CONNECTED never fired · ▲ / ? assume the worst.
  5. Confirm SAFE by the base lamps, not the screen.
  6. Disconnect and short the leads first. Then diagnose.
Danger — a pad you cannot disarm RELAY WELDED, or RELAY LIVE lit with the key in SAFE → the bus may be live with nothing commanding it. Do not approach the igniter. Reach the base from the side and power it off at the on/off switch.

End of day — not optional Disconnect both battery packs. Neither unit has a hardware undervoltage cut-off, and ERROR halts operation without disconnecting the load — a unit left switched on will flatten its LiPo. Confirm all eight channels read ○ OPEN before packing up.
