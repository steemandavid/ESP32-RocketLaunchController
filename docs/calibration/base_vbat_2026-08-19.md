# Base unit VBAT divider calibration — 2026-08-19

Reference: DVM at the board terminals, bench supply swept 4.56–12.92 V.
Firmware: `tools/vbat-cal` (GPIO 1, ADC1, 12-bit, 12 dB — same config as
`rlc_battery.c`). Raw counts mapped through this chip's own `adc_cali` curve,
captured in `base_adc_curve_2026-08-19.log`.

## Fits

| Model | Ratio | Offset | Worst-case error |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw counts, straight line | — | +689 mV | 322 mV (rejected: S-shaped residuals) |
| via adc_cali, gain-only, all points | 4.3301 | — | 146 mV |
| via adc_cali, gain+offset, all points | 4.2281 | +223 mV | 57 mV |
| **via adc_cali, gain-only, operating range (≥8.7 V)** | **4.3148** | — | **89 mV (0.70 %)** |
| via adc_cali, gain+offset, operating range | 4.1506 | +424 mV | 39 mV |

**Adopted: gain-only, ratio 4.3148.** The offset models fit better numerically
but the offset is large and physically unexplained — it is absorbing ADC
curvature with a straight line, which would extrapolate badly outside the
fitted band. `rlc_battery.c` has no offset term and does not gain one for this.

## Conclusions

1. **The divider resistors are correct.** 4.3148 fitted vs 4.3 nominal is 0.34 %,
   well inside tolerance. The divider was never the error source.
2. **The dominant error is ADC compression near full scale.** A fully-charged 3S
   pack (12.6 V) puts 2920 mV on the pin — 92 % of the ADC's calibrated ceiling
   of 3163 mV; the 12.92 V point reached 95 %. The incremental scale collapses
   from ~3.58 mV/count mid-range to 2.42 mV/count at the top.
   **A higher divider ratio (~5.5:1) would put full charge near 2.3 V and land
   the whole operating range in the linear region.** Hardware change, not firmware.
3. **Direction of residual error is conservative.** Before calibration the
   firmware under-read by 87–102 mV near the arming thresholds; after, by
   50–68 mV. It still reads low, so it blocks arming slightly early rather than late.
4. **Sampling noise is comparable to the calibration error.** Instantaneous
   spread was ~130 counts peak-to-peak (±1.7 %) on a bench supply.
   `rlc_battery.c` averages 8 single reads taken 1 s apart, leaving roughly
   ±0.6 %. An oversampling burst per sample would help. Not yet applied.
